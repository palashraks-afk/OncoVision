# Objective (recorded 18 October 2026, in Palash Rakshit's words and mine)

**Goal.** Find cancer risk early and cheaply from information people already have for free, so more cancers
are caught sooner and screening costs less, and get that into people's hands at scale. The work has to stay on
"cheap cancer risk prediction / screening".

**Three deliverables**

1. Research: pre-registered paper(s) that score at least **5 on novelty, 5 on impact and 4 on paper quality**
   (scale and scores: `docs/ANGLE_ASSESSMENT.md`). Scores are my own judgement, never moved to make numbers fit.
2. App: Oncovision, turning the research into something an ordinary person can use. The Congressional App
   Challenge is an option, not a given (its 2026 rules require the student to write the code; see below).
3. A discovery: a replicated finding that helps reach the goal and gives the app a reason to spread.

**Rules this repo holds itself to**

- Bars and analyses are committed before they are run; misses are reported as misses.
- Nothing is claimed that was not measured; no lives-saved claim without trial evidence.
- AI use is disclosed; an AI cannot be an author; a human second analyst and clinician review are still needed.
- No fabricated history, no invented stories, no secrets in the repo.

**Where things stand on 18 October 2026**

| Work | Result | Scores (novelty / impact / quality) |
|---|---|---|
| Free information and cost-matched invitation (`docs/cancer_age_paper/`) | Age, sex, smoking rank cancer death; inviting by risk covers more deaths at equal cost; fairness bar missed | 4 / 4 / 4 to 5 |
| Medicare multi-cancer test law (`docs/mced_paper/`) | Law covers about 12% of Medicare-age cancer deaths in 2029; free-question triage roughly doubles cancers found per test | 5 / 4 / 4 |
| Discovery screen (`docs/DISCOVERY_SCREEN_PREREG.md`) | Pre-registered; running | pending |

build the app, with AI allowed only for specific supported parts and fully disclosed. Most of this repo was
